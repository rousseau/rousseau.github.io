I am a professor at [IMT Atlantique](https://www.imt-atlantique.fr) and a research scientist at [LaTIM](https://latim.univ-brest.fr) (Inserm) in Brest, in the field of computational imaging. My research focuses on computational methods and machine learning for medical image analysis, with applications to the neuro-musculoskeletal system and especially early brain development: fetal and neonatal brain MRI, super-resolution reconstruction, segmentation, cortical surface analysis and modelling of brain growth. I am also working on computational imaging for ultra-low field MRI.

Software
------

Open-source Rust tools for fetal brain MRI (macOS and Linux):

* [medoxide](https://github.com/rousseau/medoxide): fetal brain extraction, slice-to-volume reconstruction and tissue segmentation
* [surfoxide](https://github.com/rousseau/surfoxide): white and pial cortical surfaces, mesh quality control and cortical measures
* [gyroxide](https://github.com/rousseau/gyroxide): GPU simulation of cortical folding by tangential growth
* [guioxide](https://github.com/rousseau/guioxide): graphical interface to follow a subject through the pipeline, from a BIDS dataset to the simulation

Team code: [github.com/gis-beachild](https://github.com/orgs/gis-beachild/repositories)
